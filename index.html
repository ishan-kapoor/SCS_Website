About SCS

A decade of trust. A new chapter of leadership.

We don't just fill positions. We help organizations build leadership teams that define the future.

For over a decade, Search Career Services has been at the forefront of India's talent acquisition landscape and is now building a global presence, partnering with organizations to identify, engage and onboard exceptional talent across industries and borders. Established in 2013, we have built our reputation through consistent delivery, deep market understanding and an unwavering commitment to successful employer–talent partnerships.

Our PAN India experience supporting startups, growth-stage companies, mid-sized enterprises and established organizations with their most critical hiring requirements is the foundation for our global presence.

Today, as the talent landscape evolves, we are entering our next phase of growth: a strategic expansion into Executive Search, Leadership Acquisition and CXO Hiring with a mission to build a global presence, helping organizations secure transformational leaders across borders.

Our vision

To build a truly global presence as a recognized Executive Search and Leadership Advisory firm, connecting exceptional leadership talent with ambitious organizations worldwide.
